This lesson covers routines: quick ones without a schedule, repeating ones with a reminder, and the week and month calendars.

## Video transcript {#transcript}

Lesson four: routines, the things you repeat every day, every week, or once a year.

### Quick add {#t-quick}

The quickest way is the quick add line on the [Daily routines](/routines) page: type, and press Enter. The routine lands in the unscheduled list.

### Schedule {#t-create}

The full form is behind the plus button: a title, a description, a category, the time, a daily repeat, and a reminder fifteen minutes before. Click Create routine to save it.

The category sets the color on the calendar, and a routine can repeat once, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Reminders work for repeating routines.

### Done {#t-done}

Done? Tick the circle. The done counter goes up, and tomorrow the routine comes back.

### Calendar {#t-week}

Week and month views put your routines on a calendar. From the terminal, `tmgr routine` lists them too:

```sh
tmgr routine list
```
