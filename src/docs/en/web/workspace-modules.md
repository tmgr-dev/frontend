This lesson shows workspace modules: how to start from a preset, turn on a single module and hide one just for yourself.

## Video transcript {#transcript}

Lesson two: modules. TMGR does a lot, and each workspace turns on only what it needs.

### Modules {#t-overview}

Modules live in [workspace settings](/settings/modules), grouped into time and focus, planning, knowledge, AI agents, integrations, and notifications.

### Preset {#t-preset}

The fastest start is a preset. Click Apply a preset and pick Personal: the preview shows what changes before anything is applied. The board, for example, leaves the menu. Confirm, and the menu gets shorter.

### One module {#t-module}

You can also turn on a single module. Turn on Workflow, which is statuses and the board, and the board is back in the menu.

Hide for me removes a module just for you, and leaves your team as it is.
