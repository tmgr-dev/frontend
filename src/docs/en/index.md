## AI agents {#ai-agents}

Connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP clients to TMGR. Your agents can work with your tasks and reach you on your phone when they need you. Start with the [AI agents guide](/docs/agents).

## Command line {#command-line}

`tmgr` lets you work with tasks from the terminal and run AI coding agents where you can watch them, stop them and undo what they did. The [command line guide](/docs/cli) shows what it can do, one scenario at a time.
